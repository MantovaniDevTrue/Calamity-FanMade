Calamity FM v21.5.4 — Surface Render Hotpath Audit
===================================================

Base: v21.5.3 Aerialite One-Time Persistence Fix
Purpose: reduce Calamity overhead on ordinary vanilla surface frames without changing gameplay, biome behavior, mining, boss AI, projectile AI, summons, damage, loot or worldgen.

Observed video evidence
-----------------------
- Sustained block breaking drops the on-screen counter into the low teens (sample: ~11 FPS at 28 s).
- When block breaking stops / death screen is shown, the counter recovers to ~61 FPS (sample: 31 s).
- This mining-specific collapse is treated as a separate external tile/mining hotpath and is not modified by this build.

Changes
-------
1. SceneEffectLoader normal-surface fast path
   - When no Calamity scene and no Calamity menu are active, skip custom sky Color.Lerp/Color.Multiply/native ColorOfTheSkies writes entirely.
   - Preserve a short 24-tick handoff after leaving a Calamity scene to avoid abrupt visual snapping.
   - Reset transition state on save/quit.

2. SurfaceBackgroundLoader visibility cache
   - Cache whether any Calamity surface background layer actually has visible alpha.
   - Far/middle custom background draw paths now return immediately on ordinary vanilla surface frames.
   - Preserve menu and biome transition drawing.
   - Skip native Color multiplication for background layers whose alpha is effectively zero.

3. Main surface background visibility update
   - Refresh the custom-background visibility cache after Terraria updates front-layer fades and Calamity applies its fade adjustments.

Notes
-----
This build deliberately does not attempt to hide or compensate for a third-party mining mod that performs synchronous work when tiles are hit/broken. The goal is to remove Calamity-only surface-render overhead so normal surface gameplay does not pay for custom biome/menu rendering while those features are inactive.
